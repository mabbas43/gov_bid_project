SECTION 1. INVITATION FOR BIDS
Provincial Education Foundation invites sealed bids from eligible firms for the assignment described below.
The estimated cost of the assignment is PKR 95,000,000.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK
1. Implement finance, HR, payroll and procurement ERP modules.
2. Migrate legacy accounting data and integrate with the provincial treasury system.
3. Provide on-site implementation across nine regional offices.

SECTION 3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
3.1 The bidder must hold a valid National Tax Number (NTN) and appear on the FBR Active Taxpayers List (ATL).
3.2 Minimum average annual turnover of PKR 40 million over the last three years.
3.3 The bidder must have been in business for at least 5 years.
3.4 Prior ERP implementation experience at two public sector organizations is preferred and carries additional marks.
3.5 SAP or Oracle partner certification is desirable.
3.6 The bidder should deploy a team of at least 25 full-time technical staff on this assignment.
3.7 Affidavit on stamp paper that the bidder has not been blacklisted by any government body.
3.8 Bid security in the form of a pay order or bank guarantee shall accompany the financial bid.

SECTION 4. BID SECURITY
Bid security of 2% of bid value shall be furnished. Bids without bid security shall be rejected.
Bid validity shall be 120 days from the date of opening.

SECTION 5. EVALUATION
The technical proposal carries 70 marks and the minimum qualifying score is 65%.
Financial proposals of technically responsive bidders only will be opened.
Award shall be made to the most advantageous bid under Rule 38 of the Public Procurement Rules 2004.

SECTION 6. CONTRACT TERMS
Performance guarantee: 10% of contract value. Payment is milestone based.
Applicable taxes shall be deducted at source as per rules.
