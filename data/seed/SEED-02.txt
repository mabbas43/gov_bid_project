SECTION 1. INVITATION FOR BIDS
Islamabad Capital Services Cell invites sealed bids from eligible firms for the assignment described below.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK
1. Deliver an Urdu and English AI chatbot for the citizen helpline on web and WhatsApp.
2. Integrate the chatbot with the existing complaint database through REST APIs.
3. Provide an administrator dashboard, staff training and six months of support.

SECTION 3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
3.1 The bidder must hold a valid National Tax Number (NTN) and appear on the FBR Active Taxpayers List (ATL).
3.2 The firm must hold valid registration with the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB).
3.3 Minimum annual turnover of PKR 20 million in the last financial year.
3.4 The bidder must have at least 4 years of business history.
3.5 The bidder must demonstrate at least one completed AI chatbot or conversational assistant project.
3.6 Affidavit on stamp paper that the bidder has not been blacklisted by any government body.
3.7 Bid security in the form of a pay order or bank guarantee shall accompany the financial bid.

SECTION 4. BID SECURITY
Bid security of PKR 240,000 shall be furnished. Bids without bid security shall be rejected.
Bid validity shall be 120 days from the date of opening.

SECTION 5. EVALUATION
The technical proposal carries 70 marks and the minimum qualifying score is 65%.
Financial proposals of technically responsive bidders only will be opened.
Award shall be made to the most advantageous bid under Rule 38 of the Public Procurement Rules 2004.

SECTION 6. CONTRACT TERMS
Performance guarantee: 10% of contract value. Payment is milestone based.
Applicable taxes shall be deducted at source as per rules.
