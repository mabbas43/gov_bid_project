BIDDING DOCUMENT
Security Operations Centre and Data Centre Hardening
Tender Reference No: SEED-06

Procurement method: Single Stage, Two Envelope, under the Public Procurement Rules 2004.
Bids shall be submitted through the e-Pak Acquisition and Disposal System (e-PADS).

Date of publication: {{PUBLISHED_DATE}}
Closing date and time: {{CLOSING_DATE}} at 11:00 AM
Bids will be opened 30 minutes after the closing time.

SECTION 1. INVITATION FOR BIDS
Directorate of Government Information Security invites sealed bids from eligible firms for the assignment described below.
The estimated cost of the assignment is PKR 60,000,000.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK
1. Establish a 24x7 Security Operations Centre with SIEM and SOAR tooling.
2. Harden data centre servers and storage, and conduct penetration testing.
3. Supply network security appliances with three year OEM support.

SECTION 3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
3.1 The bidder must hold a valid National Tax Number (NTN) and appear on the FBR Active Taxpayers List (ATL).
3.2 Minimum average annual turnover of PKR 45 million over the last three years.
3.3 The bidder must hold a valid ISO 27001 certificate.
3.4 At least two staff members must hold CISSP or CISM certifications.
3.5 Authorized OEM partner letter for the proposed SIEM is required.
3.6 Affidavit on stamp paper that the bidder has not been blacklisted by any government body.
3.7 Bid security in the form of a pay order or bank guarantee shall accompany the financial bid.

SECTION 4. BID SECURITY
Bid security of 2.5% of bid value shall be furnished. Bids without bid security shall be rejected.
Bid validity shall be 120 days from the date of opening.

SECTION 5. EVALUATION
The technical proposal carries 70 marks and the minimum qualifying score is 65%.
Financial proposals of technically responsive bidders only will be opened.
Award shall be made to the most advantageous bid under Rule 38 of the Public Procurement Rules 2004.

SECTION 6. CONTRACT TERMS
Performance guarantee: 10% of contract value. Payment is milestone based.
Applicable taxes shall be deducted at source as per rules.
