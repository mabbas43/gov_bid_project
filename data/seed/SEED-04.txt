BIDDING DOCUMENT
Campus Network Upgrade (LAN/WAN and Wi-Fi)
Tender Reference No: SEED-04

Procurement method: Single Stage, Two Envelope, under the Public Procurement Rules 2004.
Bids shall be submitted through the e-Pak Acquisition and Disposal System (e-PADS).

Date of publication: {{PUBLISHED_DATE}}
Closing date and time: {{CLOSING_DATE}} at 11:00 AM
Bids will be opened 30 minutes after the closing time.

SECTION 1. INVITATION FOR BIDS
Federal College of Technical Education invites sealed bids from eligible firms for the assignment described below.
The estimated cost of the assignment is PKR 20,000,000.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK
1. Upgrade the campus LAN and WAN, including structured cabling across six blocks.
2. Install managed switches, firewalls and campus-wide Wi-Fi.
3. Provide network monitoring, documentation and a one year warranty.

SECTION 3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
3.1 The bidder must hold a valid National Tax Number (NTN) and appear on the FBR Active Taxpayers List (ATL).
3.2 Minimum annual turnover of PKR 25 million during the last financial year.
3.3 The bidder must have been in business for at least 3 years.
3.4 The bidder must have completed at least one network infrastructure project worth PKR 10 million or more.
3.5 Affidavit on stamp paper that the bidder has not been blacklisted by any government body.
3.6 Bid security in the form of a pay order or bank guarantee shall accompany the financial bid.

SECTION 4. BID SECURITY
Bid security of 2% of bid value shall be furnished. Bids without bid security shall be rejected.
Bid validity shall be 120 days from the date of opening.

SECTION 5. EVALUATION
The technical proposal carries 70 marks and the minimum qualifying score is 65%.
Financial proposals of technically responsive bidders only will be opened.
Award shall be made to the most advantageous bid under Rule 38 of the Public Procurement Rules 2004.

SECTION 6. CONTRACT TERMS
Performance guarantee: 10% of contract value. Payment is milestone based.
Applicable taxes shall be deducted at source as per rules.
